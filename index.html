EyeFortin Ingredients

EyeFortin is formulated with a combination of vitamins, amino acids, and botanical compounds intended to provide nutritional support for everyday eye and visual wellness. Below are 7 key ingredients associated with the formula:

1

Niacin (Vitamin B3)

Niacin is an essential B vitamin involved in normal energy metabolism and nervous-system function. It helps the body convert nutrients from food into usable energy and contributes to maintaining normal cellular processes.
2

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 plays a role in normal protein and energy metabolism and supports healthy nervous-system function. Including B6 in a nutritional formula provides support for several fundamental processes throughout the body.
3

Taurine

Taurine is an amino-acid-related compound naturally present in various tissues. It participates in a range of cellular processes and is commonly included in nutritional formulations focused on overall cellular and neurological wellness.
4

L-Tyrosine

L-Tyrosine is an amino acid that the body uses to produce several important biological compounds, including certain neurotransmitters. It is included in EyeFortin as part of the formula's broader nutritional approach to neurological and everyday wellness.
5

Alpha GPC

Alpha GPC is a choline-containing compound that serves as a source of choline for the body. Choline is involved in normal cellular function and contributes to neurological processes, making Alpha GPC a common ingredient in formulas designed to support cognitive and nervous-system wellness.
6

Eyebright Extract

Eyebright is a botanical traditionally associated with eye wellness. EyeFortin includes eyebright extract as part of its plant-based component, complementing the formula's vitamin and nutrient ingredients.
7

Huperzine A

Huperzine A is a naturally occurring compound derived from Huperzia serrata. It has been studied primarily in connection with neurological and cognitive processes and is included in EyeFortin's broader nutritional formulation.

Together, these ingredients create a multi-component supplement designed to complement a healthy lifestyle and provide nutritional support for everyday eye wellness. EyeFortin is a dietary supplement, not a treatment for eye disease, and the current product label should be consulted for exact ingredient amounts and serving information.
